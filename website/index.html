Questions

Before you book.

What do I actually have to do?

Record your episode and send us the file. We handle editing, clips, captions, show notes, thumbnails, publishing and scheduling. If you want to approve episodes before they go live, we'll build that into the process.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month. Clients stay because the work is good, and some have been with us for four and five years.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how often you publish and what you need. We'll recommend a plan on the discovery call so you get an exact number for your show. DRAFT: add a "plans start at $X/mo" line if you want to pre-qualify

What is show strategy, exactly?

Before you record, we work through the questions that make an episode land: who it's for, what you want listeners to do next, which guests and topics open doors for your business, and which moments are worth clipping. You get a clearer show without adding work to your plate.

I don't have a podcast yet. Can you help?

Yes. We'll use the discovery call and strategy session to shape the show's format and audience before your first recording. DRAFT: confirm you take new shows

Why only 10 shows?

So every show gets real attention from people who know it. When the roster is full, we keep a short waitlist.
