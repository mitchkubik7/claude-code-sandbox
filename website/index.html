What do I actually have to do?

Record your episode and send us the file. That's it. We handle strategy prep, editing, clips, captions, thumbnails, publishing and scheduling. If you'd like to approve episodes before they go live, we'll build that into the process.

Who will I be working with?

The team on this page. Grant is your first call, Mitch oversees your account, and Bridget leads production. No hand-offs to people you've never met.

What does show strategy include?

Before you record, we work through the questions that make an episode land: who it's for, the one idea it should leave behind, what listeners should do next, and which moment is worth clipping. You get a sharper show without adding work to your week.

Is there a contract?

No. We're month-to-month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how often you publish and what your show needs. We'll walk through options on the discovery call and give you an exact number.

Why only 10 shows?

So every show gets real attention from people who know it. When the roster is full, we keep a short waitlist.
