FAQ

Before you book.

What do I actually have to do?

Record your episode, wherever you are. We pull the files from your recording platform and handle everything after: editing, show notes, thumbnails and publishing, plus clips and captions if your show needs them.

What does show strategy include?

Before you record, we work through the questions that make an episode land: who it's for, the one idea it should leave behind, what listeners should do next, and which moment is worth clipping. You get a sharper show without adding work to your week.

How much does it cost?

Production starts at $2,000/month. That covers full production of every episode: video and audio editing, show notes, and publishing to YouTube and every audio platform. Clips, captions and social distribution are scoped on top, based on what your show needs.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We work month-to-month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Who will I work with?

Mitch, who runs AuthentIQ day to day, and our producer Bridget. No account managers and no hand-offs.
